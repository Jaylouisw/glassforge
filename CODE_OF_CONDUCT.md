# Code of conduct

Be straightforward and be kind. Disagree with the change, not the person. Assume the other person is
competent and has a reason for what they wrote — ask what it is before rewriting it.

Not welcome: harassment, personal attacks, dismissive "just read the docs" answers to genuine
questions, and shipping someone else's work without credit.

Maintainers may edit or close contributions that ignore this. If something here is being violated,
contact the maintainer directly; reports are handled privately.
